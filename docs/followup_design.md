# Follow-up design: a field audit with conformal dispatch (FUTURE STUDY)

**Status: a future study, not part of this one.** It needs its own pre-registration, locked before any of its pairings is timed, and its own budget. Nothing in this repository implements it, and it changes no task, hypothesis, pairing or lock of the present study (paper-quality wave, 28 Nov 2026, item 14). Scope changes are the researcher's to decide (CLAUDE.md).

1. **Question.** On host-GPU pairings the model has never seen, can a dispatcher that abstains unless it is confident keep sparse-prefill regret below a stated bound? And does the prediction interval it uses hold its nominal coverage?
2. **Field audit.** Time the locked grid (five lengths, the dense arm and each sparse arm at its densities, batch 1, plus one batch-4 cell) untraced, on 8 to 12 new pairings. Each pairing is a physically distinct host CPU and GPU, rented on demand, with the same protocol, floors and minimum clean rounds as the present study.
3. **Fixed model.** BUNGA as it stands at the follow-up's lock. Any host-price correction (this wave's item 10 locates the overstatement in the idle-gap residual) is chosen and frozen on the present study's data before the lock, never on field pairings.
4. **Conformal dispatch.** The score is the signed error of the predicted sparse-minus-dense gap per cell. Split-conformal quantiles at miscoverage α are calibrated on the pairings already run (P1 to P4, and any later pairing banked before the follow-up's lock). The dispatcher picks sparse only where the interval's upper end is below zero, and otherwise dense.
5. **Exchangeability unit: the pairing, not the cell.** Cells within a pairing share a host and a session, so calibration and coverage are counted per pairing, with leave-one-pairing-out as the secondary view.
6. **Primary endpoints, fixed in advance.** (a) Coverage of the gap interval on held-out pairings at nominal 1 − α. (b) Mean and worst regret against the measured best choice per point, against a stated bound.
7. **Comparators.** Always dense; the kernel-speedup rule; the non-abstaining dispatcher; the KernelSight-style step form and the fitted sum with the same conformal wrapper. The step form and fitted sum go in because both beat the recursion on P2 and P4 here (item 9).
8. **Pre-registration content.** Pairings and their order, cells, α, the score, the calibration set, the abstention rule, the regret bound, the stopping rule, and how a failed or partial pairing is handled. All of it is hashed and stored before the first field pairing is timed.
9. **What would count against it.** Coverage below nominal by more than its binomial interval on held-out pairings. Or abstention so frequent that regret is met only by always choosing dense. Both are stated outcomes, reported either way.
10. **Not done here.** No code, no launch and no cost estimate. The design is offered for the researcher's decision, and if adopted it gets its own plan section and task IDs.
