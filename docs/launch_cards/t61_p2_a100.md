## What runs

`python3 scripts/t61_session.py run --session results/t61_p2_<UTC>` on the instance from the repo root, after deploy, preflight, the GCS write gate and `scripts/gcp_bootstrap_instance.sh`. Each step runs in its own process through `scripts/run_phase.sh`, and the first failure stops the session. On Spot it runs in blocks (`--block`), each on its own boot, and an interrupted block's rows are discarded.

## Staged on the instance before `run`

- `p1_masks/`: P1's banked XAttention masks, copied from T3.2d's step 5 (`<cell>/recording/masks.{npz,json}` for every `xattention` and `xattention_noassert` cell), for the mask gate (T2.23).
- The P1 training file at `results/p1_training/p1_training.json.gz`, its sha256 the lock's.
- `docs/lock_values.json`, the tagged lock (T4.4). Without it the session's values and the mask-gate bound are unset, and the steps refuse.
- Nothing for P1's banking time: a real run reads it, and the training file's sha256, from the pairing ledger (`predictions/P1/ledger/banked.json`) and refuses `--banked-at` (ruling 1e of 11 Nov 2026).

**Cheap checks first** (adviser's ruling 2 of the 10 Nov 2026 paste: a failure costs minutes). `prereqs`, step 1, checks every staged input a later step reads (`t61_session.p2_staged_inputs`): the lock is complete and its mask-gate bound is set, and `p1_masks/` reads back (both read by step 6); the training file's sha256 is the lock's `p1_training.file_sha256` and the learned configuration is set (both read by step 11, after about three hours). Before 10 Nov 2026 the sha256 was checked nowhere in the runner. On Spot, a block checks only what its own steps read. Then `preflight` (3 min) checks the instance, the GPU variants and lightgbm; the first long step is `probe`.

**Pre-launch CPU pass, before the yes is asked** (adviser's ruling 4 of the 11 Nov 2026 paste). On this card's commit, from a clean tree: `ATTNBENCH_PRELAUNCH_MASKS=<T3.2d's step 5 XAttention masks, staged> scripts/prelaunch_cpu_pass.sh p2 results/p1_training/p1_training.json.gz` (the masks: the third lock-wave rulings of 12 Nov 2026, item 2). It runs the session's real config and name through the real start checks (the exit wrapper, a clean tree, the timed-path pin, the banking times) and every step a CPU can run with its real body, prereqs and the staged inputs first; only the GPU steps, the device and the bucket are mocked (`tests/test_prelaunch_cpu_pass.py`). The yes is asked only after it prints PASSED at the commit the yes names, and the request cites its record (`prelaunch_cpu_pass.json`). Until the lock commit it refuses at the start: no `docs/lock_values.json`, so the timed path has no pinned value.

## Not exercised before P2 (documented risks)

The L4 rehearsal's launch 4 is the last rehearsal launch (adviser's ruling 2 of the 10 Nov 2026 paste). At best it runs steps 1 to 15 of P2's runner on P2-R once, with two blocks and a simulated reset. These are still not exercised on a GPU before P2's own session, and each is a risk P2 carries:

- The two-path mask verdict (T2.23, `real_mask_gate`): the L4 has no Triton path, so the rehearsal compares one path against P1's banked masks only.
- The A100 instance: `a2-ultragpu-1g`'s provenance, Flex-start and Spot provisioning, Spot's own preemption (the rehearsal resets an on-demand instance) and P2's four Spot blocks (the rehearsal runs two).
- The lock: the session's values from `docs/lock_values.json` (`t31_steps.fill_lock_values`), the lock's mask-gate bound and learned configuration. The rehearsal runs P1's working values and configuration 4 of `docs/learned_cv_p1_20261109.json`. Step 1 checks that they are present, on a CPU only.
- P2's full grid: lengths above 8192, batches above 1, and the Kineto trace sizes, disk and memory there. The rehearsal's grid is 2048 and 8192 at batch 1.
- The compiled groups beyond 2048 at batch 1 (scope v), and all of them if launch 4's compiled stage does not run clean. If the compiled path is not clean by 14 Nov 2026, the compiled group is dropped from P2 and P4 and the limitation is written in the plan (Section 8 calendar; ruling 2 of the tenth 9 Nov 2026 paste).
- Step 1's refusal on the plan rows not Done (the rehearsal records them and does not refuse), and the teardown and pull from the A100's zone.

## Time and cost

Cap = rate x planned hours x 1.3; halt = 1.3 x planned hours (rounded down); hard delete = the halt + 10 min. Rates are the plan's all-in planning rates (Section 5.2); `GCP_RATE_INR_HR` takes the rate of the model actually created.

**The suite record is in the setup** (adviser's ruling 1h dated 10 Nov 2026, which supersedes the earlier 10 Nov ruling that left it out): the bootstrap runs `gcp_bootstrap_instance.sh "$NAME" "$ZONE" --suite-record` on the first boot (on Spot, block b1 only), and its 20 minutes are in the row below and in every cap. `scripts/gpu_suite_record.sh` deselects the 33 tests that assume a CPU-only host (`tests/gpu_host_deselect.txt`, from `docs/gpu_suite_failures_20261110.md`). **Risk:** the selection has not run on any GPU host; a test that fails on the A100 for a reason the L4 did not show stops the bootstrap, before any step, at the cost of the setup. P2's relaunches run without it (the researcher's ruling of 12 Nov 2026, after launch 1 stopped in it); its minutes stay in every cap as margin.
