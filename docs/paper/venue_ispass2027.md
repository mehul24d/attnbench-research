**What this means here (inference, flagged).**
1. **Dates.**
   - The abstract deadline of 11 Dec 2026 falls two days after the plan's last GCP launch date (9 Dec 2026).
   - The full-paper deadline (18 Dec) is the cutoff named in this wave's previous task.
2. **Generative AI.** This project's use of AI tools for code, analysis and drafting would count as "substantial use" and must be disclosed in the acknowledgments. That conflicts with CLAUDE.md rule 9 ("no model identifiers in research artifacts") only if the disclosure must name a model. The policy as fetched does not require a name. **This is the researcher's decision.**
3. **Attendance.** An accepted paper requires one author to travel to New York; a US visa may be needed.
