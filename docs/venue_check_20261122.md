## ISPASS 2027

Sources: <https://ispass.org/2027/cfp/> and <https://ispass.org/2027/submission/>.

- **Dates (all AoE).**
  - Abstract: 11 Dec 2026. An abstract is required before a full paper can be submitted.
  - Full paper: 18 Dec 2026.
  - Rebuttal: 15–19 Feb 2027.
  - Notification: 8 Mar 2027.
  - Symposium: 9–11 May 2027, New York City. At least one author must present in person.
  - As of 22 Nov 2026 the submission site had not opened.
- **Length and format.**
  - Nine single-spaced, double-column pages, 10-point, IEEE conference template.
  - References are unlimited and do not count. Figures and appendices count within the nine.
  - PDF only, with numbered pages. A paper over the limit may be rejected without review.
  - Tool and benchmark papers have no separate limit.
- **Double-blind.**
  - Remove names and other hints of identity.
  - Cite your own work in the third person, and do not omit references.
  - Give no tool or artifact URL that reveals identity; state the intent to open-source on acceptance.
- **arXiv.** The page says: "You may submit a paper already posted to arXiv, or post your paper to arXiv after submitting it." If posting after submission, notify the Program Chair. Use different titles for different versions. Do not advertise the work on social media during review.
- **Originality.** The paper must not be published or under review elsewhere. Workshop material without copyrighted proceedings is allowed.
- **UNVERIFIED:** whether an appendix or supplementary file may be uploaded separately, and any artifact-evaluation track.

## TMLR

Sources: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html>, <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/faq.html> and <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html>.

- **Double-blind.** Submissions must be anonymised. Reviewers must not search for arXiv versions.
- **arXiv.** Authors may post to arXiv or other preprint servers "at any time, either anonymously or including their identity". The submission must not link to a version with the authors' names. A preprint does not block submission, unless it is in another venue's official proceedings. Use the style file's "preprint" option on arXiv.
- **Originality.** TMLR does not accept expanded versions of conference papers. It does not accept work published, accepted or submitted in parallel at another archival peer-reviewed venue. Non-archival workshops and arXiv do not count against this.
- **Length.**
  - There is no hard page limit; length should be justified by content.
  - Unusually long papers (not counting appendices) are likely to see reviewing delays.
  - The FAQ names a different review timeline above 12 main-body pages.
- **Implication for this study.** ISPASS (archival) and TMLR cannot run in parallel. TMLR would also refuse an extended version of an ISPASS paper. This is the policy text as read, not a ruling.
- **UNVERIFIED:** the exact review-timeline difference above 12 pages.
