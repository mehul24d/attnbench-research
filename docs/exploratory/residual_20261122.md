## 1. What the data allow

**The prediction side splits exactly.** The recursion ends on the timer's closing synchronise. So each prediction is the sum of four terms: host h, synchronisation cost s_0, queue stall and synchronisation wait. They add up to the prediction within 1e-9.

**The measured side splits once.**
- The ground truth is untraced. Taking traced device durations as the untraced ones, the measured idle time is the measured time minus the traced device busy time.
- The remaining error is then exactly the predicted idle time minus the measured idle time. The identity holds to 3e-17 s. The device chain and the traced busy time agree within 0.3% of measured.
- So **all of the 0.208 is device-idle time the model predicts and the GPU did not have.**
- Measured idle cannot be divided further into host, queue and sync. That needs an untraced host timeline, which no session records, and the traced host timeline is ~3.8x inflated (P4's dense 1951 cell: 65.7 ms traced against 17.3 ms untraced).

**Copies.** No non-synchronising copy (link time l) is priced or traced in any P2 or P4 cell. Every host-device copy is a synchronising copy to the host, so it sits in the s_0 term. The copy term is zero by construction.

The reading is that P4's residual is host pricing. The step-4 null-kernel host components overstate per-operation host cost by about 1.6x at the median, and by at least 2x for dense, mean-pool and XAttention at 1951 tokens (the oracle: 1.6x). Queue and synchronisation costs explain under 5%.

This is consistent with H1's 16 Nov miss being mostly host and launch, and it locates that miss in h rather than in Q or s_0. Why the null-kernel pass overprices h on these hosts has not been checked here. The S4 finding that null pass over recorded is 2.1–2.5x for leaf calls is one candidate.

## 4. Dispatcher margin sensitivity (post hoc)

The recursion's dispatcher picks mean-pool 0.25 at 16370 and 32700 on every pairing, and dense elsewhere. The abstaining dispatcher drops a sparse choice once the margin passes that choice's predicted advantage:

| Pairing | 16370 | 32700 |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 0.1112 (win, −13.1%) | 0.2329 (win, −23.1%) |
| P2 | 0.1124 (win, −10.5%) | 0.2930 (win, −25.0%) |
| P4 | 0.1153 (loss, +13.9%) | 0.3114 (−2.2%, inside the floor) |

- **Every margin in [0, 0.1112) gives identical choices.** That range includes 0.02264, and all three pairings score the same across it: P1 mean regret 0.0011, P2 0, P4 0.028 (worst 0.139).
- **Removing P4's loss costs P1 and P2 their 16370 wins first.** At margins 0.15 to 0.20, P1's mean regret is 0.031 (worst 0.151) and P2's is 0.024 (worst 0.118). At 0.30 every win is gone.
- **P4's loss comes from the FA3 misprice.** With the traced attention in place, P4's dense prediction at 16370 is 0.135 s against mean-pool's 0.158 s, so the dispatcher would choose dense. That is arithmetic on item 2, not a rerun.

These margins were read after P2 and P4. They describe sensitivity and fix nothing.

## Plan defaults (flagged)

- Device busy time is the union of device activities that start between the prefill's `aten::embedding` call and the end of the timer's synchronise. Where a cell has two traces, the mean is used.
- The inflation measure pools GEMM and other-device kernels. Attention is left out, because P4's dense ground truth ran FA3.
- The host scale factor multiplies every h by one factor. Its search range is [0, 4]; cells outside it are reported as unreachable (P4: 2, P2: 5).
